        How the score works

        Each measure scores 0–10 on a straight line between a “0 points” and a “10 points” value, set for the company's industry (from its SEC industry code): a 20% gross margin is poor for a software company and normal for a retailer. Pillars average their measures, then the pillars are weighted into the overall score. Banks and insurers are scored on measures that suit them. The thresholds and weights are judgement calls and haven't been back-tested against share-price returns.

        Growth 20%, profitability 25%, balance sheet 15%, dilution 10%, valuation 20%, momentum 10%. Grades: A 8+, B 6.5+, C 5+, D 3.5+, F below.

        Sources. The company's financial statements and industry code from SEC EDGAR, read when you ask; the share price from Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq or Stooq, whichever answers first. Scores are kept for up to an hour, so repeat visits are instant. Your recent scores are kept in this browser only.

        Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any company it scores. A structured summary of public numbers, not a forecast, a recommendation or investment advice. See also the in-depth Opendoor Scorecard.
